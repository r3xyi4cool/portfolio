Crystal Training

This Crystal Training Plugin is designed to enhance player training and create a dynamic in-game experience by adding unique functionalities centered around the use of totems and zombies. The plugin introduces the /train command, which provides players with the ability to strategically place totems and summon powerful enemies for combat practice.

Better Tournament

Tournament Plugin automates PvP tournaments with 1v1 and FFA modes, managing arenas via WorldEdit/FAWE and storing data in YML. It features seamless kit support, automated match handling, and winner announcements.
